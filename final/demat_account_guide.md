# Demat Account Guide for Beginners 📈

## What is a Demat Account?
- Digital locker for your shares and securities
- Just like a bank account but for investments
- Required to buy/sell stocks in India

## How to Open
1. Visit broker website/app
2. Enter PAN + Aadhaar
3. Complete KYC (video verification)
4. Get account in 24-48 hours

## Types of Orders
- **Market Order** - Buy/sell at current price immediately
- **Limit Order** - Buy/sell only at your specified price
- **Stop Loss** - Auto-sell if price falls below a level

## Key Terms
- **NAV** - Net Asset Value (mutual fund price)
- **52W High/Low** - Highest/lowest price in a year
- **P/E Ratio** - Price to Earnings (company valuation)
- **Dividend** - Company profit shared with shareholders

> Start with index funds before picking individual stocks!
